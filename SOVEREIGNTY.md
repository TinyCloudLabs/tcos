# The TinyCloud Sovereignty Commitment

> Sovereignty enables powerful technology to serve the intentions of the people
> using it.

TinyCloud releases its open-source software under the Apache License 2.0 because
sovereignty requires the freedom to inspect, use, modify, and share the systems
on which people depend.

We ask everyone who uses, operates, modifies, or redistributes TinyCloud
software to uphold these commitments:

1. Preserve meaningful user agency.
2. Make delegated authority visible, understandable, and revocable.
3. Support portability and user-controlled exit.
4. Protect the confidentiality and integrity of user data.
5. Never use control of infrastructure to hold people or their data hostage.
6. Communicate system behavior and limitations honestly.

The Apache License permits you to disregard this request. We will not pretend
otherwise. But doing so rejects the purpose for which this software was placed
in the commons. You may have the legal right to build against sovereignty; you
do not have TinyCloud's endorsement, marks, certification, or cooperation in
doing so.

If your organization cannot make this commitment, contact TinyCloud to discuss
commercial support, certification, trademark rights, warranties, or other
arrangements. A separate arrangement is not required to exercise the rights
granted by Apache-2.0.

## Legal status

This commitment is a statement of purpose and a voluntary community standard.
It is not part of the Apache License 2.0, does not add a field-of-use
restriction, and does not reduce or condition any right granted by that license.
